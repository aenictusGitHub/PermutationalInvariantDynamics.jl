# Community conduct

PermutationalInvariantDynamics.jl is a research-software community. Be
professional, precise, and respectful in issues, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces.

Expected conduct includes:

- critique code, mathematics, evidence, and documented assumptions rather than
  people;
- welcome questions and differing levels of Julia or physics experience;
- disclose conflicts of interest, copied material, unpublished sources, and
  uncertainty that affects a technical claim;
- protect private reports, credentials, and unpublished research shared for a
  legitimate review; and
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as harassing, discriminatory,
  threatening, sexually inappropriate, or persistently disruptive.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close or lock discussions,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where
necessary, and made without retaliation.

For ordinary concerns, contact the maintainers through a repository issue or
discussion. For sensitive security matters, use `SECURITY.md`. If a concern
involves a maintainer, use GitHub's private reporting and abuse channels rather
than disclosing personal information publicly.

This policy is original project text distributed under `GPL-3.0-only`; it does
not incorporate the Contributor Covenant or another external conduct-policy
template.
